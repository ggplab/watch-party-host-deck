Feature ② Spoiler blur

Spoilers get blurred the moment they’re posted

A rewatcher types about a scene that hasn’t aired yet. Gemini finds that scene and blurs the message as it arrives.

Want to see it? Click to reveal, your choice. The blur clears by itself when that scene airs.

Blurred on arrival. Click to show. Friends reply: “야야 그거 스포임”, “AI가 가려 줘서 다행”

04 How we made it instant

How we made it instant

We pre-compute 18 scene boundaries × 3 questions and cache the answers.

0.2 s

Catch me up recap

Cached. A first question takes 7.8 to 9.7 s.

0.3 s

Spoiler check

Cached. A new chat line takes 1.3 to 2.0 s.

Next: a keyword index from the scene log → milliseconds; the model only confirms.

Numbers: app session report, deployed app measured 2026-10-16© 2026 BuildnWrite. All rights reserved.
04 Built to scale

Built to scale

Each video is processed once, and every room reuses it.

More viewers do not add scene-log cost.

The costly part is checking chat; a keyword index cuts it.
