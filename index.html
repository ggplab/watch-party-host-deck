Feature ② Spoiler blur

Spoilers get blurred the moment they’re posted

A rewatcher types about a scene that hasn’t aired yet. Gemini finds that scene and blurs the message as it arrives.

Want to see it? Click to reveal, your choice. The blur clears by itself when that scene airs.

04 How it works

One broadcast clock behind both

Everyone watches what the host plays. One clock, two rules.

NOW ON AIR
Scenes already aired
Scenes still to come

Used for you

Catch-up and answers come only from scenes already aired.

Blurred until it airs

Chat about scenes not yet aired is blurred on arrival. Click to peek; it clears when the scene airs.
